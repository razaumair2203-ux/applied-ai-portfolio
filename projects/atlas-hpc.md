# ATLAS — GPU/HPC research environment

ATLAS is the CAE research compute environment I lead for GPU/HPC-supported engineering and AI work. It is included here as **infrastructure / enablement evidence**, not as a claim that I built a hyperscale cloud platform.

## Scope

- GPU/HPC environment supporting AI, simulation and engineering workloads;
- user guidance and reproducible examples;
- container patterns for research workloads;
- monitoring / administration documentation;
- controlled contribution and pull-request governance;
- small technical team supporting the environment and research users.

## Why it matters

Applied AI leadership is not only model development. Teams need reproducible compute, versioned examples, controlled environments, access discipline, monitoring and a path from experiments to repeatable execution. ATLAS provides that shared engineering layer for the current R&D portfolio.

## Evidence boundary

The production/admin repositories are not mirrored into this public portfolio. This page records the role and engineering scope only; it does not expose infrastructure topology, credentials or institutional configuration.
