# Counter-UAS Phase I — computer-vision demonstrator and research evolution

This is a public-safe summary of a private evidence archive. It intentionally separates **completed Phase I evidence** from future sensing research.

## Completed Phase I evidence

- computer-vision drone-detection demonstrator;
- indoor and outdoor detection trials;
- preserved training curves and original trial/visual records;
- hardware and camera-mount evidence;
- archived source material with file hashes, provenance and an evidence manifest.

The private archive preserves the original media and historical project artifacts rather than replacing them with generated illustrations in this portfolio.

## Research evolution — proposed, not demonstrated

Follow-on research explores how independently recorded sensing modalities could be compared and eventually fused while preserving provenance. Proposed directions include optical-motion processing, passive RF and acoustic sensing.

Those directions are **not presented as completed Phase I hardware or field capability**. The completed evidence base is the computer-vision demonstrator and its trials; the broader multisensor framework is a development path.

## Engineering value

This work adds evidence of:

- computer vision in a physical sensing setup;
- indoor/outdoor experimental trials rather than notebook-only inference;
- dataset/trial provenance and reproducibility discipline;
- transition from a single-modality demonstrator toward system-level research questions;
- explicit separation of what has been demonstrated from what is planned.

The detailed source repository remains private because it contains historical programme material and original project evidence. This page is therefore secondary portfolio evidence rather than a substitute for the inspectable public-code projects.
