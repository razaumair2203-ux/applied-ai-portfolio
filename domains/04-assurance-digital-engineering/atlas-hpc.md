# ATLAS GPU/HPC: shared compute for AI and simulation

A 22-node GPU cluster that lets researchers train, evaluate and simulate on repeatable infrastructure.

## Why it matters

Research results are only as reliable as the machines that produce them. Shared, managed compute replaces one-off workstations and undocumented local setups.

## What I did

- **Lead** research-computing operations and the technical staff who run them.
- **Integrated** the cluster into AI training, model evaluation and simulation workflows.
- **Set** operating practice for shared use across multiple research teams.

## Results

- **22 nodes** in service.
- Stack: Linux/Ubuntu, SLURM scheduling, CUDA, Docker.
- Supports AI training and evaluation, simulation and engineering research.

## Status

In service; shared by multiple research teams.
