ATLAS is a **22-node GPU/HPC research-computing environment** supporting AI, simulation and reproducible engineering workflows.

| Dimension | Evidence-backed state |
|---|---|
| Environment | **22-node** GPU/HPC research-computing environment |

| My role | accountable technical leadership for research-computing operations and associated technical staff |
| Boundary | this public portfolio does not publish infrastructure credentials, internal topology, utilization data or restricted institutional configuration |

## Engineering contribution

My responsibility is at the **system and operating-environment level** rather than presenting ATLAS as a personal model-training project. The work includes technical accountability for the research-computing environment, its use as shared engineering infrastructure, and the operational discipline needed to support reproducible AI and simulation work.

The environment strengthens the wider applied-AI programme by providing a controlled compute layer for repeated experiments, model evaluation, simulation and engineering workflows that should not depend on a single workstation or undocumented local state.

## Contribution boundary

- **Direct responsibility:** accountable technical leadership, operational oversight and integration of the environment into research/engineering workflows.
- **Shared infrastructure:** compute use, research jobs and individual projects are carried out by multiple researchers and teams and are not represented as sole-authorship outputs.
- **Public-release boundary:** node-level configuration, credentials, security controls, utilization records and institution-sensitive infrastructure details are intentionally excluded.
